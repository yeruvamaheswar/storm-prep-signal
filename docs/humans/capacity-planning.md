# Charge cheap, sell expensive

The engine already names charge, hold, or discharge from the North price. The allocator still only sells. These seven prompts make the plan follow that name: charge when the zone price is cheap, sell when it is high, hold in between.

Batteries move to the public Base-shaped pack (25 kWh, 11.4 kW). The demo tape stays 0.40 MW at 100 homes. Live price becomes all four load zones, not North only. The wall can say Charge.

The long note and the copy-paste prompts are `docs/agents/plans/capacity-planning-prompts.md`.
