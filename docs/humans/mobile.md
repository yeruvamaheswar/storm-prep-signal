# Mobile (phones)

The operator site works in **portrait** on phones (about iPhone width). Layouts reflow under a small-screen rule; the desktop look stays the same.

On Replay and Live, the map stays open. Tap **Scenario** or **Inputs**, or **This tick**, to open a panel. **Send / Keep / Trust** sits above the play bar.

Open `/flow` or Replay on your phone, wait if the API is waking up, and use the same controls as on a laptop.

Not a downloadable app. Landscape and tablet polish are out of scope.

Detail for agents: [`docs/agents/mobile-layout.md`](../agents/mobile-layout.md).
